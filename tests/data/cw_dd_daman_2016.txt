                                       NOTIFICATION

No. 7-14/EC/DD/2015 : Whereas, the Administrator of the Union Territory of Daman and Diu
had, by a notification under Rule 23 of the Daman and Diu Municipalities (Elections) Rules,
1995, and published in the Official Gazette on the 6" January, 2016 called upon all the
wards of the Daman Municipal Council to elect their respective members for the purpose of
constituting a new Municipal Council;

       And, whereas,    in pursuance   of the provisions of Rule 28 of the Daman       and        Diu
Municipalities (Elections) Rules, 1995, the Election Commission for the Union Territory of
Daman and Diu, appointed under Section 185 of the Andaman and Nicobar Islands
(Panchayats) Regulation, 1994, appointed the dates for filing nominations, taking the poll
etc with respect to the said election vide its Notification No. 7-14/EC/DD/2015, dated the 6"
January, 2016;

       And, whereas, the poll was taken on the 30" January, 2016;

        And, whereas, after the counting of votes on the 1* February, 2016, the Returning
Officer has declared the result of election in respect of each ward of the Daman Municipal
Council;
