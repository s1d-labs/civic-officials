COASTAL PS KARAIKAL
District: KARAIKAL
About

Coastal Police Station, Karaikal, functioning under Puducherry Police, plays a crucial role in strengthening coastal security, preventing maritime-related crimes and safeguarding the lives and livelihoods of coastal communities. Situated in the strategically important coastal region of Karaikal along the Bay of Bengal, the station focuses on coastal surveillance, maritime vigilance and maintaining law and order within its jurisdiction. Its key responsibilities include conducting coastal patrolling, monitoring suspicious activities, preventing illegal activities along the shoreline, assisting fishermen and coastal residents during emergencies, and coordinating with the Indian Coast Guard, Marine Police and other relevant agencies whenever required. Through regular surveillance, community engagement, coastal security awareness programmes and effective emergency response, the station strives to strengthen public confidence and promote cooperation with fishing communities. With vigilance, dedication and professionalism, Coastal Police Station, Karaikal, remains committed to protecting the coastline, ensuring maritime safety and contributing to a secure and peaceful coastal environment for the people of Karaikal.
