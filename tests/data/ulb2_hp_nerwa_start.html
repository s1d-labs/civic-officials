Nagar Panchayat Nerwa

Nerwa is a Tehsil Hedquarter in Shimla District of Himachal Pradesh State, India. It is located 126 KM towards East from District head quarters Shimla. Nerwa Pin code is 171210 and postal head office is Nerwa . Bharanu ( 5 KM ) , Chaupal ( 6 KM ) , Dhotali ( 9 KM ) , Madhana ( 9 KM ) , Mundli ( 9 KM ) are the nearby Villages to Nerwa. Nerwa is surrounded by Jubbal Kotkhai Tehsil towards North , Shillai Tehsil towards South , Sangrah Tehsil towards South , Chakrata Tehsil towards East .
