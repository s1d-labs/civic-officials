Introduction
Ward Committee No. 3 has been established as per the provisions of the Maharashtra Municipal Corporation Act, 1949, and works to assist the Hon. Municipal Commissioner in administrative affairs. Under the leadership of the Committee Chairperson, the committee undertakes coordinated planning of civic issues, local governance matters, and development works in the respective ward area.The committee actively monitors departmental tasks, issues recommendations, conducts inspections, and ensures follow-up actions, aiming to deliver efficient and responsive services to citizens.Ward Committee No. 1 acts as a crucial link between local governance and the public, playing a vital role in achieving transparent, people-centric, and result-oriented municipal administration."
