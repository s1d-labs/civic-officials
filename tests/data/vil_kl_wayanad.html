Villages

Kalpetta is the headquarters of the Wayanad District Administration with Civil Station and other main offices. The district has 3 municipalities viz, (1) Kalpetta, (2) Mananthavady, and (3) Sulthan Bathery and 4 development blocks viz, (1) Mananthavady (2) Kalpetta (3) Sulthanbathery, and (4) Panamaram comprising of 23 panchayats.
