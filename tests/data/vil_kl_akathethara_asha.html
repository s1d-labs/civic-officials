ASHA Worker Details

🏥 Akathethara Grama Panchayat - ASHA Workers

👩‍⚕️ Guardians of Community Health

ASHA (Accredited Social Health Activist) workers are the backbone of rural healthcare activities and maternal-child care. They play a crucial role in ensuring community health across every ward in Akathethara Grama Panchayat. Leading the way in immunization drives, health awareness, and epidemic prevention, the official details of the ASHA workers are provided below.

🎯 Main Objective

To ensure basic healthcare services are accessible to every rural family (especially women and children), promote health awareness, and facilitate easy access to healthcare facilities.

📋 Key Responsibilities

  • Ensure necessary awareness and care for pregnant and lactating mothers.
  • Ensure timely immunization for children.
  • Participate in preventive actions and surveys to control communicable diseases.
  • Assist local residents in reaching hospitals during health emergencies.
