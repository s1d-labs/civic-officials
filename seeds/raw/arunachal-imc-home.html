New Birth and Death Registration applications are no longer accepted through the IMC website.

All new registrations, as well as corrections to existing Birth and Death records, shall be processed through the Central Civil Registration System (CRS) Portal at:

?? https://dc.crsorgi.gov.in/crs/home

Applicants may also visit the Itanagar Municipal Corporation (IMC) Office in person for assistance regarding new registrations and correction of Birth and Death certificates.
