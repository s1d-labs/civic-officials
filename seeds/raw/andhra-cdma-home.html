Building Liveable Cities Together

Building Liveable Cities Together: The Commissioner and Director of Municipal Administration (C&DMA), Government of Andhra Pradesh, is the nodal authority coordinating with Urban Local Bodies to strengthen governance, service delivery, and city finances, thereby driving cleaner, safer, and more liveable cities.

Aligned with the Swarna Andhra Vision 2047 and its emphasis on collaborative, citizen-centric, and data-driven governance alongside sustainable urban planning, C&DMA works on building cities together with communities, Urban Local Bodies, and departments to deliver inclusive and future-ready urban growth.
