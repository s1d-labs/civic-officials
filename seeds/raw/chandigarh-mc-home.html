Municipal Corporation Chandigarh

Municipal Corporation is city beautiful Chandigarh came into existence on May 24, 1994 by the way of an ordinance. This ordinance took the shape of Act namely the Punjab Municipal Corporation Act, 1976 to as extended the U.T, Chandigarh by Punjab Municipal Corporation Law (extension to Chandigarh) Act, 1994 (Act No 45 of 1994). Municipal Corporation span over 114 sq km area. Executive Power of MCC is vested in Municipal Commissioner, an IAS Officer appointed by Chandigarh Administration. Mayor Message

I feel honoured and excited to serve as the Mayor of our City Beautiful, Chandigarh. During my time as a councillor, I was able to vote for and experience some amazing changes in the City Beautiful. I grew up in this area and now, live, work and play in Chandigarh and appreciate where we have come from and the reality of the city today. I recognise the need to remember our roots, while also thinking progressively towards the future development of the city. Chandigarh is known and loved for its beautiful city feel and welcoming presence towards those who visit or move here. As we continue to grow, I will be mindful of this and do my best to maintain that unique quaintness that is City Beautiful Chandigarh. I look forward to guiding city beautiful into the future, while keeping the best interests of all stakeholders including residents, business owners and future generations in mind.

Commissioner Message

Welcome to the Municipal Corporation Chandigarh website! At MCC, our mission is clear: to make Local Government work for all citizens of City Beautiful. Through visiting this site, it is our hope that we effectively communicate how we work to achieve this mission.

From streamlining our Municipal hiring process, to spearheading major sustainable efforts, to transforming our Municipal fleet into the cleanest in the Nation, here at MCC, we exemplify modern and effective service.

As MC Commissioner, I further pledge this Corporation’s steadfast support, collaboration, and dedication to problem-solving to improve our city for every single resident we serve. I hope as you explore our website, you see how MCC is committed to improving the quality of life for all Citizens equitably, effectively, and sustainably.
